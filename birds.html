Noon–3 pm
Laguna Atascosa trails Partner hike
Return to the refuge for the Mesquite Trail (1.5 mi) and Paisano Trail (2 mi). Go before 10 am heat peaks or wait for the slight cooling after 5 pm. Partner can run or hike independently. Target: Painted Bunting males on exposed singing perches, Groove-billed Ani, Olive Sparrow, Buff-bellied Hummingbird.

Dusk waterway walk Bonus birds
Walk the Arroyo Colorado bank at last light — Black-crowned and Yellow-crowned Night-Heron begin fishing, Green Kingfisher possible along the channel edge. Low effort, high reward. Early night — big day tomorrow.
Day 2 — Best day

Saturday, May 30

Laguna Atascosa → South Padre Island Move camp: SPI beach north of Access #5
6:00 amDawn
Laguna Atascosa — Bayside Drive + hawk platform Top session
Best morning of the trip. After the auto tour, spend time at the hawk observation platform as morning thermals develop. Aplomado Falcon territory is along the grassland edge — scan fence lines and isolated perches. The RF 200-800 at 800mm on the R7's APS-C crop (1,280mm equiv.) earns its rental fee on these distant open-country birds.
💡 Check eBird "Explore Nearby" the night before for any rare sightings at Laguna Atascosa in the last 48 hrs. Local birders post daily.
9–10 am
Break camp → drive to South Padre Island 50 min
Pack up Thomae, drive via Queen Isabella Causeway. Stop in Los Fresnos or Port Isabel for fuel and ice before the island. Deflate truck tires to ~20 psi before driving onto the beach. Drive north of Beach Access #5 and position well above the high tide line with the truck nose facing southeast into the prevailing wind.
⚠ Check NOAA tide tables before positioning — set up at least 30 ft above the high tide line. Re-inflate to road pressure before leaving Sunday morning.
11 am–1 pm
SPI Birding Center — boardwalk + 5-story tower Coastal birds
5-min drive south from beach camp. Reddish Egret doing its erratic lurching hunt (one of the most entertaining behaviors in North American birding), Tricolored Heron, Black-necked Stilt with chicks, American Avocet on the flats. 5-story tower gives panoramic Laguna Madre views — worth the climb for both of you.
📍 SPI Birding & Nature Center — Google Maps
1–4 pm
Sea Turtle Inc. + Gulf beach Partner highlight
Open Tue–Sun ✓ — walk 200 yards from the Birding Center. June is peak nesting season with maximum active rescues and rehab cases. After, Gulf beach time directly from camp — swim, walk the tide line for shorebirds, decompress in the southeast breeze.

Dirty Al's Pelican Station, Port Isabel The nice dinner

9:30 pm
Return to SPI beach camp Best night
The Gulf's southeast breeze runs 10–20 mph most May/June nights. Orient the pop-up screen side into the wind and you'll sleep meaningfully cooler than any inland site. Set the alarm for 6:15 am.
Day 3 — Last morning

Sunday, May 31

Final dawn → drive home Home to Austin by 3–4 pm

Check eBird overnight before heading over. Home by 3 pm. Import SD cards into Lightroom that evening while the trip is fresh. Run Denoise on the best Reddish Egret and raptor shots. Ship the lens rental return label Monday morning.

Pre-Trip Checklist

  • Reserve Adolph Thomae Jr. County Park Call (956) 748-2044 · Confirm late Thu night arrival · Request waterway-facing dry site
  • Order RF 200-800 lens rental from Lensrentals Ship to Austin address · Thu delivery · Include Monday return label
  • Check NOAA tide predictions for SPI June 7–8 tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov · Note high tide time before driving onto beach
  • Download eBird offline for Cameron County Laguna Atascosa has limited cell service — download the county ahead of time
  • Check BirdCast migration forecast night before each morning birdcast.info — particularly valuable for SPI Saturday dawn session
  • Pack DEET (essential — not optional) June thornscrub chiggers at Laguna Atascosa are serious. Apply to ankles + wrists before every trail session.
  • Format SD card in-camera after confirming two backup copies Canon R7 → Setup → Format Card · Never format before importing
  • Check SpaceX Starbase launch schedule Hwy 4 closures near Boca Chica won't affect SPI but good to know
  • Pack tire pressure gauge + know your beach PSI target Deflate to ~20 psi for sand · Re-inflate to road pressure before leaving beach
